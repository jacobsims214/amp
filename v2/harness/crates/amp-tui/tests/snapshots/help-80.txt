
 AMP
   /amp                     the AMP connection: server, user, MCP health
   /ticket [id|none]        the current ticket; switch to another, or clear it
   /workers                 workers here, and tickets in progress elsewhere
   /questions               tickets waiting on an answer; answer them here
   /plan                    plan approval and the epics and stories
   /project [id]            the project and its board; switch to another
   /open [id|plan]          open the ticket (or the plan) in the browser
   /server [name|address]   the AMP servers; switch to another

 Chat
   /model [provider/model]  the model; switch it for this session
   /connect [provider]      model providers and their keys; add a key
   /usage                   tokens and cost this session
   /abort                   stop the reply
   /close                   close the AMP panel
   /help                    this list
   /quit                    leave the chat

 Keys
   Enter                    send
   Shift+Enter              new line
   /                        commands: ↑↓ choose, Tab complete, Enter run
   Esc                      stop the reply, or close a panel or the menu
   Ctrl+C                   stop the reply, clear the editor, or quit
   Ctrl+D                   quit (with an empty editor)
